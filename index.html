ABOUT ELEVIQ

A platform built around the student's next step.

ELEVIQ is an educational and opportunities platform focused on helping students and young people discover and access admissions, examinations, scholarships, bursaries, grants, registrations and other useful opportunities.

Our long-term goal is simple: reduce the stress of searching for educational opportunities by bringing useful information and practical assistance together in one place.
